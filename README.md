# Free Certification Course

A curated directory of free and conditionally free learning credentials, digital badges and certificates.

## Scope

AI · Python · software engineering · cybersecurity · cloud · DevOps · networking · mathematics · physics · electronics · RF · embedded · robotics · CAD · communications · data · databases · QA · OS · open source · quantum · technical education · business technology · healthcare · environment · and other professional fields.

## Rules

- ✅ **0 ₽** — free under normal access conditions on the issuer's current official page.
- ⚠️ **Condition** — student/educator status, voucher, scholarship, campaign, partner programme, or course-specific condition.
- ❌ **Paid** — not counted as a free credential.
- A **badge is kept as a badge**, a course-completion certificate as a certificate, and a professional exam certification as a certification.

## Editorial approach

The catalogue is source-first. We do not label a course as a free certificate unless the issuer's own current materials support that claim. When a credential is only free under a condition, it is marked ⚠️.
